Anthem of the Seas
Ship Overview
Class: Quantum Class | Year Built: 2015 | Capacity: 4,180 guests
Anthem of the Seas is a modern ship that combines cutting-edge technology with adventure activities. The ship features the North Star, an observation capsule that rises 300 feet above sea level for panoramic views. Other highlights include RipCord by iFLY, an indoor skydiving simulator, and SeaPlex, the largest indoor activity space at sea with bumper cars, roller skating, and circus school.
Key Features:
Two70° - A multi-purpose entertainment venue with floor-to-ceiling windows and digital screens
18 dining options including specialty restaurants like Jamie's Italian and Wonderland
Broadway-style theater productions
Indoor pool with retractable glass roof
Virtual balconies in interior staterooms

Summer 2026 Itinerary: Alaska Adventure
7-Night Roundtrip from Seattle
Ports of Call:
Day 1: Seattle, Washington (Departure)
Day 2: Cruising the Inside Passage
Day 3: Juneau, Alaska - State capital with access to Mendenhall Glacier
Day 4: Skagway, Alaska - Historic Gold Rush town, home to the White Pass Railway
Day 5: Sitka, Alaska - Russian heritage site with excellent wildlife viewing
Day 6: Victoria, British Columbia - British-influenced city famous for Butchart Gardens
Day 7: Cruising
Day 8: Seattle, Washington (Return)
Best For: Nature enthusiasts, adventure seekers, and travelers interested in glaciers, wildlife, and frontier history. Expect cooler temperatures (50-65°F in summer).
