Icon of the Seas
Ship Overview
Class: Icon Class | Year Built: 2024 | Capacity: 5,610 guests
Icon of the Seas is Royal Caribbean's newest and largest ship, designed as the ultimate family vacation destination. The ship is divided into eight distinct neighborhoods, each offering different experiences. It features the largest waterpark at sea, multiple pool areas, and over 40 dining and bar venues.
Key Features:
Category 6 - The largest waterpark at sea with six major waterslides
Thrill Island - Includes Crown's Edge, a suspended obstacle course over the ocean
Surfside - Dedicated area for young families with water play and activities
The Hideaway - First suspended infinity pool at sea
Aquadome - Three-story entertainment venue with waterfall features and live shows
Over 40 restaurants, bars, and entertainment venues

Typical Passenger Profile
Age Range: All ages, with heavy emphasis on families with children (ages 2-17)
Passenger Types:
Families with young children and teenagers
Multi-generational groups (grandparents to grandchildren)
Young couples in their 20s-40s
Friend groups seeking activities and nightlife
First-time cruisers

Summer 2026 Itinerary: Eastern Caribbean
7-Night Roundtrip from Miami
Ports of Call:
Day 1: Miami, Florida (Departure)
Day 2: Cruising (Day at Sea)
Day 3: Philipsburg, St. Maarten - Dual-nation island with duty-free shopping and beaches
Day 4: Charlotte Amalie, St. Thomas - US Virgin Islands with shopping and Magens Bay beach
Day 5: Cruising (Day at Sea)
Day 6: Perfect Day at CocoCay, Bahamas - Royal Caribbean's private island with waterpark
Day 7: Cruising (Day at Sea)
Day 8: Miami, Florida (Return)
Best For: Families with children, groups, beach lovers, and travelers wanting a resort-style cruise with tropical weather (80-90°F) and extensive onboard activities.
